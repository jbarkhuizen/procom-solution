Delivery

Most items ship directly from our warehouse partner within 2–5 business days of payment; we email your tracking number when your order is dispatched.

Changed your mind? (7-day cooling-off)

Under section 44 of ECTA you may cancel an online purchase within 7 days of delivery without reason. Items must be returned unused, complete and in their original packaging. The only charge is the direct cost of returning the goods. Refunds are paid within 30 days of cancellation.

Faulty or not as described

Under section 56 of the CPA, you may return a defective item within 6 months of delivery for a repair, replacement or refund — your choice — unless the fault was caused by misuse. Many products also carry a manufacturer’s warranty beyond this; we will help you claim.

Damaged in transit

Please inspect your parcel and report damage within 48 hours with photos. We will arrange a replacement at no cost to you.

How to return

  1. WhatsApp or email us with your order number and the reason.
  2. We send return instructions (usually via PUDO).
  3. Once the item is received and checked, we refund to the original payment method.
