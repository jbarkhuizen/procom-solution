This website, www.procomsolutions.co.za, is operated by Lapanza (trading as Procom Solutions), Pretoria, Gauteng (“Procom”, “we”, “us”). Contact: procompretoria@gmail.com · 082 663 9608.

We respect your privacy and process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number and delivery address when you order or contact us.
  • Order details (products, amounts, delivery method).
  • Payment confirmation references from Payfast — never your card details.
  • Basic technical data (e.g. IP address) in server logs for security.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order.
  • To answer your enquiries.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

Only what is needed to fulfil your order: our warehouse/supplier partners (name, phone and delivery address, so they can ship to you directly), courier and PUDO services, Payfast for payment, and our email provider. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser’s local storage to remember your cart, checkout details and theme preference. We do not use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as required by tax law (currently five years), then deleted.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing, by contacting us at the details above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
