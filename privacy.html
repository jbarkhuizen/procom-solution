2. What we collect, and why

We only collect what we actually need to run the shop, talk to you, and keep the site working. Here's every point personal information reaches us:

Placing an order

Name, email, phone number, delivery address, and any order notes you add. You check out as a guest — we don't create customer accounts or store passwords for customers. Used to process and deliver your order, send order emails, and (for large items) send you a delivery quote.

Payment

Card and Instant EFT payments are handled entirely by Payfast — we never see or store your card or banking details. We do receive and keep a record of the payment confirmation Payfast sends us (amount, payment method, and a payment reference) against your order, for accounting and fraud/dispute purposes.

Contact form and WhatsApp

If you use our contact form, we collect your name, email, phone (optional) and your message, and use them to answer you. If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and the messages you send, and use them to answer your question or handle your order.

Website security

Like most websites, our server keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and fix problems. We don't use these logs for marketing.

3. Cookies & similar technology

We keep this deliberately minimal — we don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies, so we don't run a cookie-consent banner. Here's exactly what's used:

  • Session cookie — used only for our own admin area, to keep staff logged in. It is strictly necessary, contains no readable personal information (just a random session token), and isn't set for shoppers.
  • Local browser storage (not a cookie, but similar) — used to remember your shopping cart, the checkout details you've entered, and your light/dark theme preference. This stays on your device and isn't sent to us unless you check out.
  • Google Fonts — this site loads typefaces directly from Google's servers, which as a normal part of serving a font receives your device's IP address. We don't control what Google does with that; see Google's own privacy policy for detail.

4. Who we share your information with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the business — we don't sell or rent your information to anyone, ever. Each of the following only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Our warehouse partner — most orders ship directly from our supplier's warehouse, so they receive your name, phone number, delivery address and the items ordered, to pack and dispatch your parcel.
  • Courier services — receive your name, phone number and delivery address, to deliver your parcel and send you delivery updates.
  • Payfast — processes your payment. Receives your name, email, and order amount to complete the transaction.
  • Google (Gmail) — we send order confirmations and notification emails through Gmail's infrastructure, so your email address and the email content (which may include your name and order details) passes through Google's systems as part of sending it.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) — if you contact us on WhatsApp, your phone number and messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp platform.

Some of these providers (Google, Meta) may process or store information on servers outside South Africa as part of their normal global infrastructure. Where that happens, it's only for the specific purposes listed above, with those providers' own standard security and data-protection commitments in place — we don't otherwise transfer your information out of South Africa or share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to comply with a legal process, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we reasonably need it: for as long as needed to fulfil an order and handle any warranty/returns period after it, and beyond that where we're legally required to keep financial/accounting records (currently five years for tax purposes). Website backups are kept on a rolling basis — only the most recent ones are kept, and older ones are automatically removed. If you'd like your personal information removed sooner, contact us and we'll action it, subject to anything we're legally required to keep on record (e.g. completed order/financial history).

6. How we protect it

Our admin area requires a separate authenticated login; staff passwords are never stored in readable form (only as a one-way cryptographic hash), session cookies are set to be inaccessible to page scripts (httpOnly), and admin login attempts are rate-limited against brute-force attempts. As with any online service, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, proportionate steps for a business our size to keep your information safe.

7. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that's inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete personal information we no longer have a valid reason to keep.
  • Object to us processing your information for direct marketing at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with us directly, or with South Africa's Information Regulator, if you believe we've mishandled your information.

To exercise any of these, email our Information Officer at procompretoria@gmail.com. We'll respond as promptly as we reasonably can.

You can also contact the Information Regulator directly: inforegulator.org.za, complaints via POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, general enquiries via enquiries@inforegulator.org.za or toll-free on 0800 017 160.

8. Children

This website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent.
